Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 30, 2025

1. Introduction

BlockSensys is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website or use our services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, and other details you provide via forms (e.g., contact or survey forms).
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with our website, such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited.
  • Health-Related Data: Symptom information entered into our AI Diagnostic Assistant, used solely for preliminary triage (not stored).

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Provide and improve our services, including AI-driven diagnostics.
  • Respond to inquiries and schedule Zoom calls.
  • Analyze website usage to enhance user experience.

4. Data Security

We use blockchain-based encryption and industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • With your consent (e.g., for scheduling Zoom calls).
  • To comply with legal obligations.

6. Your Rights

You may:

  • Request access to or deletion of your personal information.
  • Opt out of communications by contacting us at contact@blocksensys.com.

7. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at contact@blocksensys.com.
