Disruptive Technology Adoption Model (2019)

  • Subjective Norm

    Subjective Norm reflects users beliefs about managing their Personally Identifiable Digital Identity data, impacting their intention to adoption.


    Autonomy

    • Definition: Ability to manage ones digital identity without intermediaries.
    • Impact: Users value the empowerment of sovereign data management, anytime, at their discretion.
    • Challenges: Current multi-step verification processes (in-person and online) are not effortless, reinforcing the desire for autonomy.
    • Outcome: Autonomy significantly influences Behavioral Intention to adopt BDIMS.

    Cyber-Security

    • Definition: Need for enhanced security in digital identity management.
    • Impact: Users prioritize a highly secure system due to concerns about data breaches and cyber-attacks.
    • Context: Sensitive transcript data (e.g., name, address, SSN, grades) increases demand for robust security.
    • Outcome: Cyber-security concerns drive Behavioral Intention, supported by qualitative findings and past data breaches.

    Perceived Ease of Use

    Perceived Ease of Use focuses on the simplicity and efficiency of the BDIMS, influencing adoption through user-friendly design and processes.


    User Interface

    • Definition: The visual layout and functionality of the BDIMS system.
    • Impact: Users prefer a simple design with minimal pages, no nested menus, and transactions completed in two clicks or fewer.
    • Evidence: Research in Human-Computer Interaction supports that user-friendly interfaces enhance adoption.
    • Outcome: A streamlined interface drives Behavioral Intention to adopt BDIMS.

    Request Process

    • Definition: The process of requesting official transcripts.
    • Impact: BDIMS offers a faster, easier process compared to legacy systems, which involve rigorous, multi-step identity verification.
    • User Feedback: Users value consolidating transcripts from multiple institutions and controlling distribution (e.g., to employers).
    • Outcome: A simplified request process significantly influences Behavioral Intention.

    Conclusion

    The adoption of BDIMS is shaped by Subjective Norm (Autonomy and Cyber-Security) and Perceived Ease of Use (User Interface and Request Process). These factors highlight users desire for secure, autonomous, and user-friendly systems to manage their digital identities effectively.
