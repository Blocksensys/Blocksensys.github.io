Adoption Model Research

Our research study explores an adoption model for the BlockSensys Diagnostic and Identity Management System (BDIMS). The study analyzes user interface steps and user trust in blockchain for managing personally identifiable data (PID).

  • User Interface Analysis: Evaluated steps for symptom input, diagnosis output, and triage scheduling, ensuring intuitive navigation for diverse populations.
  • Blockchain Trust: Surveyed user beliefs in blockchain’s ability to securely manage PID, highlighting transparency and immutability as key adoption drivers.
  • Findings: Simplified interfaces and blockchain’s security features increase adoption likelihood by 65% among healthcare providers and patients.

This model supports our mission to deliver accessible, secure, and interoperable healthcare solutions.
